# Legendary & Mythical Pokemon Catalogue

All catchable legendaries and mythicals in Pokemon Buddy for Claude.

**How to catch:** Only available from `+100 XP` awards (ship to production, major deployments).
- Legendary — **4% chance** per production ship
- Mythical — **1% chance** per production ship

If one appears, it's automatically caught and added to your party.

## Tips

- You can only catch a legendary/mythical when **you ship to production** — that's the only `+100 XP` trigger.
- The rarer the Pokemon, the rarer the engineer mindset it represents.
- Catching **Arceus** (1% of 1%) means roughly 1 in 10,000 production ships. Frame-worthy.
