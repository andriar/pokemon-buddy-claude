Usage:
- `/poke:elite` — show Elite Four roster + next matchup
- `/poke:elite <trainer>` — challenge one: `lorelei`, `bruno`, `agatha`, `lance`, `champion`

Sequential — must defeat in order. Requires all 8 gym badges. Costs 1 battle stamina. Beating all 5 unlocks the 🏆 Champion title.

Run `python3 "${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/buddy-update.py" elite $ARGUMENTS` and print the output verbatim.
